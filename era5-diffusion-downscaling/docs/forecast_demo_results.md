## Reading guide

- **Cost-benefit, not superiority.** The 1.5° product is 36× smaller than 0.25° (240×121 vs 1440×721). Native still wins RMSE; the cheap path recovers most of the bicubic→native gap and beats native on CRPS. Both products come from the *same* 9 km run, so this is a **dissemination/bandwidth** saving, not a compute saving.
- **Two CRPS wins.** vs bicubic from the *identical* 1.5° input (pure model contribution), and vs native 0.25° from 36× less input. A deterministic baseline has zero spread by construction, so the definitive probabilistic test is IFS ENS 0.25° vs IFS ENS 1.5° + downscaling.
- **Consistency is metric-dependent** (no-geo arms): DDNM (λ=1) wins RMSE; dropping the projection (λ=0) wins CRPS at long lead because the hard projection pins every member to the same erroneous coarse field. DDNM+ (λ_t) is a null: forecast error is *structured* and lives in the range space.
- **Transports at ratio 6:** trained on the discrete set {2,4,8}, so 6 is still off-distribution; both lose to guided diffusion on the control, SI badly.
- **Residual diffusion** is projected once on the *composed* field (per its original `reconstruct_residual`), not per step — not strictly like-for-like on consistency.
