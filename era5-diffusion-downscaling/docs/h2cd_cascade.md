# Hierarchical cascade diffusion (H²CD) — charter

Status: **parked for the follow-up paper**. Not thesis-paper scope (the
thesis is the measured method anatomy; this is a new process requiring
training from scratch). This doc exists so the idea is preserved with its
gates and honest prior-art position while the thesis is written.

## The falsifiable question

Does a diffusion process whose forward dynamics erase fine scales first and
coarse scales last — so that reverse-time generation is a coarse-to-fine
stochastic cascade — beat standard VP diffusion + DDNM projection at equal
training and sampling budget on downscaling metrics (L2, spectrum,
coherence, CRPS), and does it deliver its predicted step-count reduction?

## Construction (condensed)

Decompose the field on a nested hierarchy: orthogonal detail projectors
`B_0 … B_J` (parent-average pyramid; `B_j x` has zero mean inside every
level-(j−1) parent cell; `Σ B_j = I`). Give each band its own noise clock
`β_j(t)` with staggered activation `τ_J < … < τ_0` (fine bands diffuse
first). Forward SDE `dX = −½ G_t (X−μ) dt + Q_t^{1/2} dW` with
`G_t = Σ β_j B_j`, `Q_t = Σ β_j C_j`, band covariances `C_j` (simplest:
`c_j² B_j`). Closed-form marginals via per-band `α_j(t)`; bandwise Tweedie
`x̂_0 = μ + Φ_t^+ [x_t − μ + Σ_t s_θ]` with clipped pseudoinverse for
inactive bands. Preconditioned score loss with `Σ_t^{1/2}` weighting (zero
signal for not-yet-active bands — consistent, since the reverse SDE never
queries the score there). Zero-shot downscaling: A appears only in the
inference-time DDNM correction `x̄_0 = x̂_0 + A†(y − A x̂_0)`.

**Conservation property:** while only bands finer than k are active,
`P_k X_t = P_k X_0` exactly — the forward process destroys subgrid detail
while exactly preserving all coarser parent averages.

Self-consistency note: unlike the fibre-bridge proposal, the score is
TRAINED under this forward process, so there is no frozen-model/process
mismatch (the failure mode the cov-init experiment measured).

## Prior art (the honest position)

The claim must be scoped against, at minimum:
- **WSGM** — Guth, Coste, De Bortoli, Mallat (2022), *Wavelet Score-Based
  Generative Modeling* (arXiv:2208.05003): per-scale wavelet cascade
  diffusion, coarse-to-fine, orthogonal detail spaces. The closest prior;
  H²CD is essentially WSGM with the wavelet tree replaced by a
  parent-average tree (HEALPix or block pyramid) + DDNM inference.
- **Subspace Diffusion** — Jing et al. (2022, arXiv:2205.01490): diffusion
  progressively restricted to nested subspaces with exact preservation of
  the complement.
- Blurring diffusion (Hoogeboom & Salimans, arXiv:2209.05557 — equivalent
  to per-frequency schedules), IHDM (arXiv:2206.13397), cascaded diffusion
  (Ho et al.), DLWP-HPX (arXiv:2311.06253) for the spherical UNet, DDNM
  (arXiv:2212.00490).

Defensible novelty: cascade diffusion on a **conservation hierarchy**
(equal-area parent averages, exact parent-mean preservation) rather than a
wavelet frequency hierarchy, applied to operator-agnostic zero-shot
downscaling. NOT defensible: "a new stochastic generator" — the generator
family is WSGM/subspace-class.

## The key observation: exact operator alignment on the plane

On lat-lon patches with A = block average, choose the hierarchy to be the
**dyadic block-average pyramid** (built from the repo's own
`coarsen`/`upsample_nearest` at ratios 2, 4, 8, 16). Then, for any dyadic
ratio r, `range(A†A)` is EXACTLY the span of bands coarser than log₂ r
(on HEALPix vs a lat-lon CMIP6 grid this alignment is only approximate —
grid boundaries don't coincide, and the leakage `‖(I−P_k) A†A‖` is a
measurable degradation). Consequences on the plane:

1. **Band-diagonal projection:** DDNM clamps the observed bands to y and
   touches nothing else — projection and cascade never fight.
2. **Principled mid-chain start:** for ratio r, every reverse step before
   the finer clocks activate is determined by y; skip them. This is the
   schedule-derived version of Shu et al.'s intermediate start, with the
   start time read off the clock ladder per ratio.
3. **Exact multi-ratio zero-shot:** training never fixes r; every dyadic
   ratio is a band boundary of the same trained model. Non-dyadic ratios
   are approximate (regrid first).

Sharpest claim: *cascade diffusion whose hierarchy is generated by the
degradation-operator family itself, making zero-shot downscaling a
band-clamping operation.* The planar version is the cleaner theory AND the
cheaper experiment; HEALPix is the (approximate-alignment) spherical
generalization.

## Gate ladder (cheapest first; each gate can kill the rest)

1. **Band-energy analysis (CPU, free):** decompose ERA5 test patches on
   the block pyramid; per-band energy + per-band recoverable structure
   (reuse coherence/memgap diagnostics). If the free bands' energy profile
   gives staggered clocks nothing to exploit, stop.
2. **Planar cascade prototype (decisive):** dyadic block-pyramid cascade
   on t2m — new q_sample/loss/sampler on the EXISTING data pipeline and
   UNet (~days of code + one training run). Compare at equal budget vs the
   standard VP model + projection (Table A2 row). Also measure the
   mid-chain-start step-count curve (quality vs NFE).
3. **HEALPix round-trip gate** (`data/healpix_roundtrip_check.py`, already
   written on the hpx-pilot branch): remap floor must sit well below
   method differences at target ratios. Only relevant if gate 2 wins.
4. **Full spherical H²CD** with a DLWP-HPX-style UNet + measured band
   leakage ‖(I−P_k)A†A‖ for real CMIP6 grids. Only after 1–3 pass.

## Priors from measured results (what to expect)

- FOR: the project's organizing principle — the live levers are
  information injection and schedule — and H²CD is precisely a
  schedule-space intervention.
- AGAINST (tempering): matched-HEALPix encoder parity showed grid geometry
  was not the bottleneck at 28 km; prediction: the planar prototype
  captures most of any gain, the spherical layer adds little at this
  resolution.
- NEUTRAL: the cov-init "guidance needs high-frequency energy" result does
  not apply (self-consistent training), but it is the cautionary tale for
  any shortcut that pairs this sampler with the existing white-noise
  checkpoint — don't.

## Caveats

- Hard projection preserves the resolved input's biases: this performs
  downscaling, not bias correction. Compose with the two-stage pipeline
  (bias-correct / anomaly-train, then downscale) exactly as the thesis
  README states.
- Hyperparameter surface is real: τ_j ladder, per-band c_j (or graph-
  Laplacian ν_j, κ_j on the sphere), clock shape. Budget tuning honestly
  in gate 2 or the equal-budget comparison is meaningless.
- Novelty survives only with the prior-art scoping above; cite WSGM and
  subspace diffusion prominently or a reviewer will do it for you.
